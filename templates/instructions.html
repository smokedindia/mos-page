Instructions

In this experiment, you will watch a Ground Truth (reference) video, followed by several generated sample videos. Your task is to rate each sample based on how similar it is to the Ground Truth video.

Rating Scale:

1 - Very Dissimilar: The sample is very different from the Ground Truth
2 - Dissimilar: The sample has noticeable differences from the Ground Truth
3 - Neutral: The sample is somewhat similar to the Ground Truth
4 - Similar: The sample is quite similar to the Ground Truth
5 - Very Similar: The sample is almost identical to the Ground Truth

What to Consider:

- Visual content and scene composition
- Audio quality and speech characteristics
- Audio-video synchronization
- Overall quality and naturalness
